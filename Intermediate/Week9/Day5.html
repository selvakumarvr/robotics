Key Concepts

3D PCB Preview & BOM

🎮

3D Viewer

KiCad has a built-in 3D viewer! Assign 3D models to components to see exactly how your PCB will look

📋

BOM

Bill of Materials: list of every component with part number, value, quantity, supplier. Essential for ordering parts!

🛒

LCSC / Mouser

LCSC is JLCPCB's component store — tight integration. Mouser/DigiKey for professional sourcing

💡

JLC Assembly

JLCPCB can also solder SMD components onto your PCB — PCB + Assembly service for complex boards
