The Technology

What is
Arduino?

Arduino is the world's most popular platform for learning electronics and robotics. It's used by professionals, students, and makers in over 180 countries — and it's perfectly designed for ages 10 and up.

Think of it as a tiny programmable brain you wire into your robot. Kids write code, connect sensors and motors, and watch their creation come to life.

Arduino

⚡ Used by NASA, LEGO, and thousands of schools worldwide. Open-source, affordable, and endlessly fun to build with.

How It Works

A 10-Week Journey
from Zero to Robot Builder

Every week builds on the last. Kids who've never touched electronics leave with full project portfolios and the confidence to keep creating.

1
Weeks 1–2: Foundations
Electronics basics, first LED blink, intro to Arduino IDE
2
Weeks 3–5: Sensors & Logic
Buttons, motion sensors, if/else coding, reading inputs
3
Weeks 6–8: Motion & Control
Motors, servo arms, wireless control via Bluetooth
4
Weeks 9–10: Final Project
Kids build and present their own original robot creation
